The Vriksha Story

More Than a Restaurant

Vriksha The Flavour Hub is a vibrant food and beverage destination on Yelahanka Main Road, Bengaluru — created to bring great food, diverse flavours, hospitality and memorable experiences together under one roof.

Our journey brings together two established food concepts — Red Bucket Biryani and Irani Nawabs — while giving Vriksha its own identity as a lively destination where food, people and experiences come together.

From everyday dining and chai conversations to celebrations and group gatherings, Vriksha is designed to be a place you return to for the flavour and stay for the experience.

Our Philosophy

Food brings people together.

We believe every meal can become a memory, every conversation can become an experience, and every flavour should leave an impression.
